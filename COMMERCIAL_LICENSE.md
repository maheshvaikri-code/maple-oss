## When You Need a Commercial License

The AGPL-3.0 requires that if you run MAPLE as part of a network service (e.g., a SaaS product or internal API), you must make your complete application source code available to users of that service under AGPL-3.0.

You need a commercial license if **any** of the following apply:

- You are building a **proprietary product** that uses MAPLE and do not want to open-source it
- You are deploying MAPLE as part of a **SaaS platform** and cannot or do not want to disclose your source code
- Your organization has a **blanket policy against AGPL dependencies**
- You are integrating MAPLE into an **enterprise internal system** and need a standard commercial software agreement
- You want **indemnification, SLA, or enterprise support terms** not provided by open source

## FAQ

**Q: Can I evaluate MAPLE in a proprietary context before purchasing?**
A: Yes. You may use MAPLE for internal evaluation and proof-of-concept work for up to 90 days without a commercial license. Production deployment in a proprietary context requires a license.

**Q: Does my open-source project need a commercial license?**
A: No. If your project is licensed under AGPL-3.0 or another OSI-approved open source license that is compatible with AGPL-3.0, you can use MAPLE under the open source terms at no cost.

**Q: We are a university / non-profit. Do we need a commercial license?**
A: Academic research, teaching, and non-profit internal use is permitted under AGPL-3.0 without a commercial license. If you publish a paper using MAPLE, we would appreciate a citation.

**Q: What counts as "network use" under AGPL?**
A: Under AGPL-3.0, if users interact with your application over a network (e.g., an HTTP API, a web UI, a WebSocket service), that constitutes "conveying" under the license, and you must provide the complete corresponding source code. If MAPLE is used in a pure batch/offline process with no network-accessible interface, standard GPL interaction rules apply instead.

**Q: Do you offer volume discounts or multi-year pricing?**
A: Yes. Contact us for multi-year agreements and volume pricing for large deployments.
