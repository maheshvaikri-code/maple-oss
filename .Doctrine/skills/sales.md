# Skill: Sales

**Scope.** ICP, outreach, demos, pricing scenarios, and the CRM record —
go-to-market without fiction. Drafts and models only; the human closes.

## Principles
- The ICP is written down before any outreach exists. "Anyone who pays"
  is not a profile, it is the absence of one.
- Research precedes contact: their stack, their words, their public pain.
  An email that could go to anyone will convert as if it did.
- A demo is a rehearsal, not a gamble: FDE-scripted scenarios
  (`roles/fde.md`) rehearsed to hash-stable output, never live
  improvisation on a prospect's time.
- The CRM is a record, not a mood board: prospects quoted verbatim,
  outcomes recorded as they happened.
- The honesty law closes deals slower and keeps them longer: no invented
  references, no roadmap promises, no numbers without artifacts.

## Defaults
- ICP doc: segment, trigger event, disqualifiers, and the alternative
  they'd otherwise choose — revisited when win/loss data contradicts it.
- Outreach drafts: short, specific to the research, one ask. Queued for
  the human to send (.Doctrine.md §5); the skill never contacts anyone.
- Demo script pinned to a tagged build; environment reset documented;
  the demo run twice clean before it ever meets a prospect.
- Pricing scenarios as models with assumptions listed line by line —
  the price decision is the human's, every time.
- Lost deals get an honest reason field (their words, not our comfort)
  and feed the G0 intake for product review.

## Do
- Disqualify early and record why; a fast honest no beats a slow maybe.
- Quote the prospect verbatim in CRM notes — "too expensive" stays "too
  expensive", not "budget timing".
- Answer feature questions from the CHANGELOG; absent features are "not
  today" with the request logged, never "soon".
- Script the demo's failure paths too: what to say when the scenario
  breaks is written before it can.

## Don't
- Don't send anything — email, LinkedIn, follow-up, calendar invite.
- Don't promise roadmap; a dated commitment belongs to the human alone.
- Don't paraphrase-to-flatter in the CRM; the record outlives the mood.
- Don't demo unshipped builds or live-patch mid-call.
- Don't model pricing without writing the assumptions above the numbers.

## Review checklist
- [ ] ICP on record; this prospect matches it or the exception is noted
- [ ] Research attached: their stack, their words, their public pain
- [ ] Outreach draft short, specific, one ask — queued for the human
- [ ] Demo scripted against a tagged build, rehearsed to hash-stable
- [ ] Pricing scenario lists assumptions; decision left to the human
- [ ] Lost-deal reasons recorded verbatim and routed to G0

## Common failure modes
Spray-and-pray outreach wearing a personalization token; the live demo
that improvises into a bug; CRM notes that launder rejection into
optimism; roadmap promised in the heat of a good call; pricing anchored
on hope instead of modeled assumptions; lost deals filed under "timing"
until the pattern G0 needed becomes invisible.
